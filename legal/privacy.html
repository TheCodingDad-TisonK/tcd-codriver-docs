Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-25

This policy explains what personal data TCD Co-Driver collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what your rights are. It describes what the software really does.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is TheCodingDad (Tison Klomp), an individual based in the Netherlands. Contact details are in the legal notice. For any privacy question or request, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

2. What we collect and why

  • Account. Your email address and your password. The password is never stored as text: only a salted hash (scrypt) is kept. We also keep when the account was created and confirmed. Why: to create your account, let you sign in and send you service emails. Basis: performing the contract with you.
  • License. Your key, its type, start and end dates and the number of computers allowed. For each computer that uses a key: an anonymous code made from the computer's Windows identifier by a one-way hash (we cannot read the identifier itself), the game (ETS2 or ATS), the plugin version, and when it first and last checked in. Why: to run the license, to keep a key on its allowed number of computers and to prevent misuse. Basis: performing the contract, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.
  • Payments. The note you enter when you say you paid (a PayPal transaction ID, or the name or email on your PayPal account) and when you sent it. The payment itself is made on PayPal; the details of the payment (such as your name, email, amount and transaction ID) are held in our PayPal account, not in our own database. We do not see card or bank details. Why: to check the payment and extend your key, and to keep records the law requires. Basis: performing the contract, and legal obligations.
  • Messages. If you use the contact form: your name, email address, the topic and your message. Why: to answer you. Basis: our legitimate interest in answering questions, or performing the contract.
  • Activity records. A short record of important actions, such as sign-up, key activation, payment decisions and emails that could not be sent, with the account they belong to. Why: security and fixing problems. Basis: legitimate interest.
  • Technical data. When you use the service, your IP address is used briefly in the server's memory to limit abuse (for example too many sign-in attempts). It is not stored in our database. If the server is reached through a network service such as Tailscale, that service handles the connection.

Your logbook, driving scores and plugin settings stay on your own computer. TCD Co-Driver does not send them to us. If we ever add online history, it will be optional and this policy will be updated first.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data.

3. Cookies

The license site sets two cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work: sid keeps you signed in (up to 14 days) and csrf protects forms against forgery (up to 30 days). We do not set any other cookies. The public landing page on GitHub Pages sets none of its own; GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses as described in its own privacy statement.

4. Who else receives data

  • PayPal, which processes your payment under its own privacy policy.
  • An email provider that delivers our service emails (confirmation links, password resets, expiry notices, replies).
  • GitHub, which hosts the public landing page.
  • Tailscale, if the server is reached through its network.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on the safeguards described in their own policies. We share only what is needed for each purpose.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, key, computer codes and payment notes: until you delete your account. Deleting your account from your account page removes them straight away.
  • Contact messages: until handled, then deleted after 12 months. If you delete your account we also delete messages sent with your email address.
  • Activity records: up to 12 months.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as payment records in our PayPal account: as long as the law requires.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive a copy of the data you gave us in a common format, and to withdraw any consent you gave. You can delete your account yourself at any time. For anything else, use the contact form; we reply within one month. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the data protection authority: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. The license server issues signed license tokens, limits sign-in attempts, locks an account after repeated wrong passwords, and keeps its admin tools on a separate port that is only reachable from the computer it runs on. When the service is public it is served over https. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Children

TCD Co-Driver is not meant for people under 16. If we learn that we hold an account of someone under 16 without a parent's permission, we will delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you on the website and, where we have your email, by email.
