# Licensing and payments

> **Where things live:** the public overview and prices are on the [landing page](https://thecodingdad-tisonk.github.io/tcd-codriver/), accounts and keys are on the license site linked there, and these guides are in [this repo](https://github.com/TheCodingDad-TisonK/tcd-codriver-docs).

## Trial

Create an account on the website and confirm your email. You get one free **14-day trial key**. The 14 days start the **first time you use the key in the plugin**, not when you sign up. A trial key works on one computer and must be activated within 60 days.

## Subscription

After the trial it is **$3 per month**. Payments are handled by hand through PayPal:

1. Pay $3 to the PayPal.me link shown on your account page.
2. Press **"I have paid"** on the account page and enter the PayPal transaction ID.
3. The payment appears on the owner's admin page. Once it is checked in PayPal, the key is extended by 30 days and you receive an email.

There is no automatic renewal and no automatic charge. If you stop paying, the key simply expires.

## Offline use

The plugin checks the key with the license server when the game starts and every few hours. A working license keeps running **offline for up to a week** between check-ins. If the server is briefly unavailable, your driving is not interrupted.

You will get one warning email a few days before the key ends, and one when it stops.

## Your files

Your license, settings and logbook are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TcdCoDriver`. Deleting your account on the website removes the key, activations and related data.

## Honest limit

A license check inside a program running on your own computer can always be patched out. The signed, short-lived tokens, per-computer binding and server-side decisions make casual sharing not worth it, but they cannot stop someone determined to crack the plugin. Updates, support and any future online features are the real value of a license.
